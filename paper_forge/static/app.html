Preview and confirm, then open the PR

The papers are saved on a local branch cut from origin/main. Nothing is pushed and no PR exists until a developer and a tester have seen the papers running in the app, and you allow each step below.

1. Save on a local branch (not pushed)

2. Preview, then sign-off

A developer runs the backend and frontend on the branch. A tester tests the papers. Two different people. If the papers change afterwards, both sign-offs must be given again.

3. Commit, push and open the PR

Needs both sign-offs. Each step is allowed separately. Leave a box unticked and that step and everything after it will not happen.
